# Privacy Policy for BrainCipher

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what data the BrainCipher Android application ("the App")
processes and how. The App is a single-player puzzle game.

## Data we store on your device

The App stores your game progress locally on your device using Android app storage:

- points, coins, puzzles solved, streaks, best times and other statistics
- solved puzzles, saved/favorite quotes, unlocked subjects and themes
- app settings (theme, font, difficulty, haptics, timer, frequency bar)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share a result. It is removed
when you uninstall the App or use "Reset All Game Progress" in Settings.

## Data we do not collect

We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, transmit, sell or share personal
data such as your name, email address, phone number or contacts. The App does not
require an account.

## Third-party services

- **Google AdMob** serves optional rewarded advertisements. The App requests
  **non-personalized ads only**. Google may still process limited data (such as IP
  address, device identifiers and ad interactions) to serve ads, prevent fraud and
  measure performance. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Games Services** (optional). If you choose to sign in, Google processes
  your Play Games profile and leaderboard scores under your Google account. See
  https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can use the App without signing in.
- **Android system services** (e.g. the share sheet) only run when you explicitly
  invoke them.

## Your choices

- Turn off ads by not requesting rewarded content; ads are never shown unprompted.
- Decline Play Games sign-in; all gameplay works offline.
- Clear all local data in Settings, or uninstall the App.
- On Android, reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in system settings.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated"
date above.
